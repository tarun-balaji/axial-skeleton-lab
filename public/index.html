On a practical, the structure will not come with a label. Your job is to orient the specimen, locate the region, distinguish nearby structures, and identify what has been tagged. This activity gives you repeated practice doing exactly that.

How each scene works
  1. Rotate, zoom, and pan the specimen freely. Numbered tags mark structures, just like pins on a practical.
  2. Assign a term to every tag, then press Check Labels. Correct answers lock in. Incorrect answers are flagged, but the correct name is not shown.
  3. Reorient and retry. After two misses on a tag, an optional clue unlocks. It describes relationships, not names.
  4. Practical Check: the camera moves to a new orientation and you type names with no word bank.
  5. Prove it: explain the visual or spatial clue you would use on a different specimen.
  6. When every scene is done, open Submission, download your submission PDF, and upload that one PDF to D2L.
