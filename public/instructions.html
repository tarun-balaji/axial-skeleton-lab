Complete all 13 items

There are 12 full scenes: Typical Vertebra; Adjacent Vertebrae; Typical Cervical Vertebra; C1: Atlas; C2: Axis; Cervical vs Thoracic vs Lumbar; Sacrum; Thoracic Cage; Sternum; Rib; Coxal Bone; Bony Pelvis. The thirteenth item is Quick ID: Hyoid.

You may visit scenes in any order and return to them during this open attempt. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. Use the orientation guide before identifying a structure. Reset View restores the starting orientation. Recenter Model brings a panned model back into view.

  1. Labeling: select a term for every numbered tag, then press Check Labels. Correct answers lock. Incorrect answers are flagged without revealing the correct name. Reorient and retry. After two misses on a tag, an optional clue becomes available.
  2. Practical Check: press Start Practical Check. Type the names of the lettered structures without a word bank, then press Submit Practical Check. Retry flagged items until all are correct. Rotate freely.
  3. Prove it: press Continue to Prove it. Answer each prompt in your own words using two or three sentences that explain anatomical relationships. The 25-character minimum unlocks the button; it does not guarantee full credit. Press Submit Reasoning.
  4. Evidence image: once the scene is complete, inspect its saved image. Rotate to a useful view and press Update Evidence Image if needed. This image goes into your PDF.
  5. Hyoid Quick ID: type the bone's name, press Submit, and then Finish. This item has no labeling or reasoning stage.

Download, check, and submit

  1. Open Submission. All 13 items must show a check mark. Return to any incomplete item.
  2. Press Download Submission PDF. Save the file, named HW7-your-name.pdf, and keep the activity page open until you verify it.
  3. Open the PDF. It should contain 14 pages: one cover, 12 full scene pages, and one hyoid page. Check your name; 13 of 13 items complete; all applicable rows marked Complete; readable evidence images; and all your Prove it answers. The hyoid's labeling and reasoning entries correctly say n/a.
  4. Go to D2L > Assignments > HW7: 3D Practical Prep. Attach one PDF and press Submit by the posted deadline.
  5. Check D2L's submission confirmation and attachment. Keep a copy of your PDF.

If something goes wrong

  • Model blank: keep this page open, check your connection, and wait. Contact your instructor before refreshing or switching browsers.
  • Model off screen or tag faded: use Recenter Model or Reset View; rotate to bring the far-side tag into view.
  • Button disabled: answer every required item. Reasoning answers need at least 25 characters each.
  • Progress not recorded: keep the page open, reconnect, and press Retry recording. You can still generate the PDF from the open page. Submit that PDF to D2L.
  • PDF fails: keep the page open, check your connection and browser download permissions, and try Download Submission PDF again. Contact your instructor before the deadline if it still fails.
  • Page already closed or refreshed: the old attempt cannot be recovered. If you downloaded a complete PDF, submit it. Otherwise start a new attempt and contact your instructor if this prevents timely completion.

Before the practical, practice identifying the structures on unlabeled physical specimens from multiple orientations without notes or a word bank. Use your missed structures to guide further practice.
